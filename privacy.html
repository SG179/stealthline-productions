Privacy Policy

Important: This is a starter policy based on the information provided: Google Analytics is used, advertisements are planned but not yet active, and there is currently no contact form. Review it against the actual website configuration before publishing.

1. Who operates this website

This website is operated under the name Stealthline Productions (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, use the contact details published on our Contact page.

2. Information collected

When you visit the website, Google Analytics may collect information about how the website is used. Depending on the Analytics settings and implementation, this can include pages viewed, interactions and events, approximate location, referral information, browser and device information, and online identifiers such as cookies. We do not intentionally ask visitors to enter financial account credentials or sensitive personal information.

The calculator's calculations are performed in your browser by the page's JavaScript. The calculator itself does not need to send the investment amounts you enter to a server to perform the calculation. However, review your Analytics configuration to ensure form values or other personal information are not being sent as event parameters or page data.

3. Why information is used

Analytics information is used to understand website traffic and usage, identify errors, and improve the website. Do not use Analytics to collect information that directly identifies a visitor.

4. Google Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visits and measure interactions. Google processes information according to its own terms and privacy documentation. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to restrict or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website functions. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Advertising

Advertising is planned but is not currently active on this website. If advertising is introduced, third-party advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize ads. We will update this policy and implement any required notices, consent choices, and controls before enabling advertising.

6. Sharing and processing

Analytics information is processed by Google to provide its analytics service. Depending on Google's infrastructure and settings, information may be processed in countries outside your own and may be subject to the laws of those countries. Review Google's documentation for details.

7. Retention and security

Analytics retention depends on the settings selected in the Google Analytics account. The site operator should confirm and document the actual retention setting before launch. We use reasonable steps appropriate to this small website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Contact forms and accounts

This website currently has no contact form, user account system, or payment processing. If these features are added, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

9. Children's privacy

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not intend to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the details on the Contact page so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to withdraw consent. Contact us to make a privacy request. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new update date.
