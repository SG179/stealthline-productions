How Does Compound Interest Work?

Compound interest means you earn returns on your original investment and on the returns you've already earned. Over long periods, this can significantly increase your investment value.

How We Calculate Investment Growth

This calculator uses monthly compounding. Your annual expected return is divided into monthly rates, and your monthly contribution is added at the end of each month.

Important Information

This calculator provides estimates based on your selected annual return. Actual investment returns fluctuate and are never guaranteed. Results do not account for taxes, investment fees or inflation.
