HOW THE CALCULATOR WORKS

Calculation Methodology

The Investment Growth Calculator estimates a future balance using the values entered by the visitor. It uses monthly compounding and assumes each monthly contribution is made at the end of the month.

Inputs

  • Initial investment: starting amount at the beginning of the projection.
  • Monthly contribution: amount added at the end of each month.
  • Investment period: number of years, converted to months.
  • Expected annual return: assumed annual percentage, converted to a monthly rate by dividing by 12.

Monthly calculation

Monthly rate = annual return ÷ 12.

For each month: new balance = previous balance × (1 + monthly rate) + monthly contribution.

Displayed results

  • Projected final balance: balance after the final month.
  • Total money invested: initial investment + (monthly contribution × number of months).
  • Investment gains: projected final balance − total money invested.
  • Total ROI: investment gains ÷ total money invested × 100.

Total ROI is a cumulative percentage over the full period. It is not an annualized return.

Important assumptions and exclusions

  • The assumed return is constant; real markets do not produce smooth, fixed returns.
  • Contributions are assumed to be made at month-end.
  • Taxes, account charges, investment fees, inflation, withdrawals, and changes in contribution amounts are not included.
  • The calculator does not model a particular security, fund, portfolio, or investment account.

These estimates are illustrative only. Read the Disclaimer before relying on any result.
