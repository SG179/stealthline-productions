# Investment Growth Calculator

A free, easy-to-use investment calculator that estimates how your money could grow over time through compound interest and regular monthly contributions.

## Features

- **Initial Investment:** Enter the amount you are starting with.
- **Monthly Contributions:** Add how much you plan to invest each month.
- **Investment Period:** Choose how many years you want to invest.
- **Expected Annual Return:** Enter your estimated yearly investment return.
- **Projected Final Balance:** See how much your investment could grow to.
- **Total Contributions:** See how much money you personally invested.
- **Investment Gains:** View the estimated growth from investment returns.
- **Growth Chart:** Visualize your investment growth over time.

## How to Use

1. Open the Investment Growth Calculator.
2. Enter your initial investment.
3. Enter your planned monthly contribution.
4. Choose your investment period.
5. Enter your expected annual return.
6. View your projected investment results.
